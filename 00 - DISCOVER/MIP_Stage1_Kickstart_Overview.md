**Status:** Internal Working Draft v0.1 — names not final.
**Read this first** for Kickstart, then the Onboarding SOP and Delivery SOP.

---

## What this stage is
- **Position:** the entry point — a live introductory training workshop, before Walk. Readiness **Level 1**.
- **Character:** a facilitated **~90-minute Copilot workshop** (+ a short leadership debrief) that gets people using Copilot Chat safely and shows the value first-hand.
- **Tool:** delivered via the **NCA training platform** — a browser-based facilitator app (Azure Static Web App); no local install.
- **Delivery role:** a **facilitator** (presenter). *(Historically delivered by Nick Chubb, who is moving roles — his delivery notes will flesh these SOPs out.)*
- **Goal:** participants leave knowing what Copilot is good/bad at, can write a decent prompt, understand the ground rules, and want to do more.

## What happens
- The account manager flags an interested client; the session gets scheduled and set up in the training platform (branded to the client).
- A live workshop: how Copilot works, prompting basics, hands-on "Now You Try" exercises, debrief.
- Wrap-up: the **AI Responsible Use Pledge**, take-home assets, and a readiness-signal debrief for leadership.
- Natural lead-in to the AI Readiness Assessment / Walk.
