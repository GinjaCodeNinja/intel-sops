## The framing
NCI assesses Copilot readiness on a **five-level scale.** Each level reflects how well your Microsoft 365 environment is organized, governed, and secured — and what Copilot can safely do for your team at that stage.

**Level 1 is the normal starting point** — it simply means the environment hasn't been organized with AI in mind yet. Most organizations are here. The question isn't where you are today — it's how quickly you can get to **Level 3**, and what needs to happen to get there.

## The five levels
1. **Ready to Start** — Your Microsoft 365 environment works well for daily use, but it hasn't been organized with AI in mind yet. Some cleanup and governance work is needed before Copilot can operate safely. *The most common starting point.*
2. **Building the Foundation** — Core security is strong and the foundational work is underway: establishing who owns what, how documents should be organized, and the rules for sharing and access. Copilot is the near-term goal.
3. **Ready to Pilot** *(activation target)* — Your environment meets the baseline requirements for responsible Copilot use. A pilot group can activate and begin using Copilot in their daily work. **This is what the readiness work is designed to reach.**
4. **Copilot Active** *(comes next)* — Copilot is live for the full organization. Your team is trained, usage is monitored, and the environment is maintained to support continued AI use.
5. **Optimizing for AI** *(comes next)* — Copilot is embedded in how your team works. The focus shifts to automation, deeper workflows, and expanding what AI contributes to the business.

## What Level 3 unlocks — the activation milestone
At Level 3, a pilot group can activate Microsoft 365 Copilot and begin using it in daily work. The consistently highest-value use cases for leadership teams:

- Summarize email threads
- Recap missed meetings with action items
- Draft documents and communications
- Search intelligently across your files

## Copilot status at each level
| Level | Copilot status | What becomes available |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — Ready to Start | Not yet activated | — |
| 2 — Building the Foundation | Not yet — preparing | Groundwork underway; user training may begin |
| 3 — Ready to Pilot | **Pilot group active** | Summarize emails · recap meetings · draft documents & communications · intelligent file search |
| 4 — Copilot Active | All users | Full Copilot in Outlook, Teams, Word, Excel · meeting notes · AI-assisted document review |
| 5 — Optimizing for AI | Full capability | Copilot Pages · workflow automation · custom AI agents |

## Pace
Getting from **Level 1 to Level 3 is achievable in 8–10 weeks.** The rate-limiting factor is not technical — it's organizational. Decisions about who owns which information, how it should be classified, and what your AI policy will say require input from your leadership team. **NCI facilitates, drafts, and configures. You make the decisions.**
