Time
~10 minutes in the room
Purpose
Vocabulary building — not a full inventory
For the facilitator: Walk through this together — don't hand it out and wait. The goal is a quick, conversational sketch of their world. Rough answers are fine. This is about familiarity with concepts, not completeness.
What we're doing here: We're going to identify your 4–6 most important business processes and take a quick look at the data that supports each one. This helps us understand what Copilot would actually be working with — and starts the conversation about who's responsible for keeping that information in good shape. We're not trying to map everything today. We're building a shared picture we can refer to as the assessment progresses.
