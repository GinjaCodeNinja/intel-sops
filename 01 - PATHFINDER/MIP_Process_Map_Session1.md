# Core Process Map
## A quick look at how the business works — and where AI fits in

> **Lives in:** `NCA_Process_Map_Session1.html` — the polished facilitation aid (+ its printable twin). This is the readable in-hub copy.

*This handout predates the current program naming — it was "Session 1" of the old "Your AI Advantage" flow; it now supports the Pathfinder AI Readiness Assessment conversation. Kept faithful to the styled original it links to.*

**Purpose:** vocabulary building — not a full inventory. Used live (~10 minutes in the room). Identify the client's **4–6 most important business processes** and take a quick look at the data behind each — so we understand what Copilot would actually be working with, and start the conversation about who keeps that information in good shape.

> *For the facilitator: walk through this together — don't hand it out and wait. The goal is a quick, conversational sketch of their world. Rough answers are fine. This is about familiarity with concepts, not completeness.*

## "Open to Copilot?" legend
- **✓ Yes** — first thought: we want Copilot working in this space.
- **? Not sure yet.**
- **✕ No** — keep out.

> *Facilitator note: a "Yes" here doesn't mean yes to everything within that area. It's a starting position, not a full access decision. What Copilot can and can't see within each area gets worked out properly during the cleanup cycle. Flag this before anyone fills in the column.*
