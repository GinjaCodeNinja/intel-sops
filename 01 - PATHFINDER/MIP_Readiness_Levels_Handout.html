AI Readiness Levels:
What They Mean and Where You're Going

Before we look at your assessment results, it helps to understand the framework used to score them — and what you're building toward.

Facilitator note: Walk through this before revealing the client's level. The goal is to normalize Level 1 and make Level 3 feel achievable — not far off. This framing should land before any number is shown.

NCI assesses Copilot readiness on a five-level scale. Each level reflects how well your Microsoft 365 environment is organized, governed, and secured — and what Copilot can safely do for your team at that stage.

Level 1 is the normal starting point — it simply means the environment hasn't been organized with AI in mind yet. Most organizations are here. The question isn't where you are today — it's how quickly you can get to Level 3, and what needs to happen to get there.

The Five Levels
1
Ready to Start
Your Microsoft 365 environment works well for daily use, but it hasn't been organized with AI in mind yet. Some cleanup and governance work is needed before Copilot can operate safely. This is the most common starting point.
2
Building the Foundation
Core security is strong and the foundational work is underway — establishing who owns what, how documents should be organized, and the rules for sharing and access. Copilot is the near-term goal.
3
Ready to Pilot
Activation target
Your environment meets the baseline requirements for responsible Copilot use. A pilot group can activate and begin using Copilot in their daily work. This is what Stage 2 is designed to reach.
4
Copilot Active
Comes next
Copilot is live for the full organization. Your team is trained, usage is monitored, and the environment is maintained to support continued AI use.
5
Optimizing for AI
Comes next
Copilot is embedded in how your team works. The focus is on automation, deeper workflows, and expanding what AI contributes to the business.
What Level 3 Unlocks — the Activation Milestone

At Level 3, a pilot group can activate Microsoft 365 Copilot and begin using it in their daily work. These are the use cases that consistently show up as highest-value for leadership teams:

Summarize email threads Recap missed meetings with action items Draft documents and communications Search intelligently across your files
Copilot Status at Each Level
Level Copilot Status What Becomes Available
1 — Ready to Start Not yet activated —
2 — Building the Foundation Not yet — preparing Groundwork underway; user training may begin
3 — Ready to Pilot Pilot group active Summarize emails  ·  Recap meetings  ·  Draft documents and communications  ·  Intelligent file search
4 — Copilot Active All users Full Copilot in Outlook, Teams, Word, and Excel  ·  Meeting notes  ·  AI-assisted document review
5 — Optimizing for AI Full capability Copilot Pages  ·  Workflow automation  ·  Custom AI agents

Getting from Level 1 to Level 3 is achievable in 8–10 weeks. The rate-limiting factor is not technical — it is organizational. Decisions about who owns which information, how it should be classified, and what your AI policy will say require input from your leadership team. NCI will facilitate, draft, and configure. You make the decisions.
