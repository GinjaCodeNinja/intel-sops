# A Few Things to Think About Before We Meet Again
## Between-Sessions Reflection Guide

> **Lives in:** `NCA_Reflection_Guide_BetweenSessions.html` (+ its printable twin) — the polished client handout. This is the readable in-hub copy.

*This handout predates the current program naming — "Stage 2 / Your AI Advantage / Session 1 → Session 2" ≈ the Pathfinder onboarding **Kickoff → Report Review**. Kept faithful to the styled original it links to.*

**Use this:** after the Kickoff, before the Report Review. ~20–30 minutes, whenever suits. **Jot notes — there are no wrong answers.**

**This is not a form to fill in correctly.** It's a set of questions worth sitting with before your assessment results arrive — so that when they do, you're already thinking in the right direction. While NCI reviews your environment, these are the questions only **you** can answer, because they're about your business, your priorities, and your comfort level. Rough notes beat silence.

## 1. What would you NOT want Copilot to be able to read?
Start here — it's often easier to define the boundaries than the access. Files, conversations, or types of information that feel off-limits, sensitive, or simply not relevant to what AI should do for your team. *There are no wrong answers.* (e.g., HR files, compensation discussions, anything client-sensitive, executive email threads.)

## 2. Where does time disappear in your week?
The tasks that repeat, the questions people ask over and over, the work that shouldn't require a human. You don't need to know anything about AI to answer — just think about friction. These are exactly where Copilot tends to help most.

## 3. Who do your people go to for "the real answer" about something?
Every organization has informal sources of truth — the person who actually knows where the current version of a document lives, or who really has the final say on a client decision. **These people are often your natural data owners.** Note who comes to mind for each area.

## 4. Who should be responsible for keeping each area's information in good shape?
Doesn't have to be final or perfect — a name or a role is enough; "unsure" is useful information too. For each area, note a **likely owner (name or role)** and whether you're **confident or unsure**:

| Area | Likely owner (name/role) | Confident? |
|---|---|---|
| Sales & Business Dev | | Y / unsure |
| Client & Account Mgmt | | Y / unsure |
| Operations & Delivery | | Y / unsure |
| Finance & Accounting | | Y / unsure |
| People & HR | | Y / unsure |
| Other | | Y / unsure |

## 5. What's your biggest concern about AI in your business — be honest?
Not the polished answer — the real one. Concerns about staff reaction, data privacy, compliance exposure, or simply not knowing what you don't know are all worth naming. **The Report Review is a better conversation when we know what's actually on your mind.**

## 6. How will you know this has been worth it? What does success look like in 6 months?
Not a KPI exercise — a gut check. If you talked to your future self six months from now and this had gone well, what would you say happened? What changed? What got easier? (e.g., the team spends less time on admin; you can get a briefing on any client file in two minutes; there's a clear policy and nobody's worried about doing the wrong thing.)

## What happens with these notes?
Bring them to the Report Review — or just bring your thoughts. There's nothing to submit. The assessment tells us what your environment looks like technically; these notes tell us what matters to **you** as a leader. Both matter. If something comes up that you want to flag beforehand, reach out to your NCI Account Manager anytime.
