This is not a form to fill in correctly. It's a set of questions worth sitting with before your assessment results arrive — so that when they do, you're already thinking in the right direction.

Your AI Readiness Assessment is underway. While NCI is reviewing your environment, there are a few questions that only you can answer — because they're about your business, your priorities, and your comfort level. Spend a few minutes with these before Session 2. Rough notes are better than silence. You don't need complete answers — just a starting point.

1
What would you NOT want Copilot to be able to read?

Start here — it's often easier to define the boundaries than the access. Think about files, conversations, or types of information that feel off-limits, sensitive, or simply not relevant to what AI should be doing for your team. There are no wrong answers.

2
Where does time disappear in your week?

Think about the tasks that repeat, the questions people ask over and over, or the work that feels like it shouldn't require a human. You don't need to know anything about AI to answer this — just think about friction. These are exactly the areas where Copilot tends to help the most.

3
Who do your people go to when they need "the real answer" about something?

Every organization has informal sources of truth — the person who actually knows where the current version of a document lives, or who really has the final say on a client decision. These people are often your natural data owners. Jot down who comes to mind for each area.

4
Who should be responsible for keeping each area's information in good shape?

This doesn't have to be final — and it doesn't have to be perfect. A name or a role is enough. If you're genuinely unsure, write "unsure" — that's useful information too.

5
What's your biggest concern about AI in your business — be honest?

Not the polished answer. The real one. Concerns about staff reaction, data privacy, compliance exposure, or simply not knowing what you don't know — these are all worth naming. Session 2 is a better conversation when we know what's actually on your mind.

6
How will you know this has been worth it? What does success look like in 6 months?

Not a KPI exercise — just a gut check. If you had a conversation with your future self 6 months from now and this AI rollout had gone well, what would you tell yourself happened? What changed? What got easier?

What happens with these notes?

Bring them to Session 2 — or just bring your thoughts. There's nothing to submit. These questions are designed to make our next conversation more useful to you. The assessment will tell us what your environment looks like technically. These notes tell us what matters to you as a leader. Both matter.

If something comes up as you work through these that you want to flag before Session 2, reach out to your NCI Account Manager anytime.
