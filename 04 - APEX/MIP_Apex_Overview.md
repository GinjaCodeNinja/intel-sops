## What this stage is
- **Position:** the **Custom Build & Optimization** stage — the top of the journey. Readiness **Level 5+**.
- **Character:** **engagement-based** (scoped per initiative, additive), running **on top of a continuing Outpost + Frontier base**. Highest stakes in the program.
- **Tools:** Copilot, Claude, and other third-party AI; MCP/API integrations.
- **Delivery role:** an **AI Expert** (scopes/designs) **+ a build resource** (builds integrations/agents).
- **Goal:** extend AI beyond M365 — connect line-of-business systems, let well-governed autonomous systems run parts of the business, and keep optimizing what's already built.

## What happens
- Per-initiative **engagement loop**: scope → design → build → **gate** → deploy → verify.
- **Custom AI agent/solution development and integration** (native / MCP / API), **advanced & autonomous agents** (heavier gate stood up at Frontier, used here), and the **managed digital workforce** (registry, monitoring, lifecycle).
- **Ongoing optimization and roadmap planning** — a standing thread, not just one-off builds: watching what's in production, tuning it, and planning what's next.
- **On the radar (placeholder):** custom AI-integrated web apps, for workflows agents alone can't carry.
