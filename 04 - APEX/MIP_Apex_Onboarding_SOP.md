# Apex — Onboarding SOP *(placeholder)*
## AI Discovery Journey · Apex Stage (Custom Build & Optimization) · For the AI Expert + build team

**Status:** Internal Working Draft v0.1 — **placeholder.** EOS-style (20/80, point form). Points marked `⟨PENDING → Register §X⟩` await the weekly group — see `NCA_Apex_Open_Decisions.md`.
**Delivery team:** AI Expert scopes/designs; a build resource (PS/dev) builds integrations & custom agents. Exact model ⟨PENDING → Register §B1⟩.
**What this covers:** the setup to stand up a client's **first Apex engagement** — the Custom Build & Optimization stage (integrations, custom/autonomous agents, managed digital workforce, ongoing optimization). Apex is **engagement-based** and runs **on top of a continuing Outpost + Frontier base.**
**Boundary:** Onboarding confirms the Frontier handoff and scopes the *first* engagement. Delivering each engagement — and the standing optimization/roadmap rhythm — lives in the Delivery SOP.
**Stance:** Consult-construct — we design, build, and de-risk; the client approves, owns, and is accountable for what goes into production.

---

## 1. Purpose & boundary
- Confirm a client is Frontier-ready, then get the first Apex engagement scoped and approved.
- Apex = **Rail A Stage 6 build work** + **Rail B B5–B6 acted on**; MI components Systems Integration, Advanced Agents, AI Operations, Digital Workforce Orchestration. Readiness **Level 5+**.

## 2. Trigger & entry
- Trigger: a Frontier client's **Ready-for-Apex checklist** is complete (`NCA_Frontier_Onboarding_SOP.md` §7) — tools graduated, integration map done, governance groundwork stood up — and the client has a concrete first build in mind. ⟨signals → Register §A1⟩
- **Confirm the Outpost + Frontier base continues underneath** — nothing turns off. ⟨→ Register §A3⟩
- Confirm exec sponsor + a technical point of contact on the client side.

## 3. Confirm the Frontier handoff *(don't skip — Apex is highest-stakes)*
- Verify the Frontier Ready-for-Apex checklist item by item. If anything's incomplete, route back to Frontier rather than starting a build on ungoverned ground. *(framework §3, §9.)*

## 4. Scope the first engagement
- Pick the first Apex initiative (an integration, a custom agent, or a **custom AI-integrated web app** — placeholder, approach TBD) — ideally high-value, bounded, and on already-governed data.
- Produce an **Apex SOW / scope doc**: outcome, scope, price, boundaries, who approves. ⟨template → Register §I1⟩ ⟨pricing → Register §A2⟩
- **Pricing note:** the SOW is quoted on top of the client's standing AI Consulting Pricing Model price — it is not folded into the formula.

## 5. Provisioning & access
- Access to the **target LOB system(s)** agreed and scoped (admin-approved consent). ⟨→ Register §C3⟩
- Build/dev resource assigned. ⟨→ Register §B1⟩
- Registry opened for deployed integrations/agents. ⟨→ Register §F3, §G2⟩

## 6. Set the optimization/roadmap cadence
- Agree how often the standing "what's running, what needs tuning, what's next" review happens. ⟨cadence → Register §F1⟩ This is a recurring thread, not a one-off part of the first engagement.

## 7. Ready-for-Engagement checklist *(handoff to the Delivery SOP)*
- [ ] Frontier handoff confirmed (tools, integration map, governance)
- [ ] Data/workspaces in scope are signed off (or catch-up backlog opened)
- [ ] First engagement scoped (SOW), priced, and approved
- [ ] Target LOB system access agreed
- [ ] Build/dev resource assigned
- [ ] Optimization/roadmap cadence agreed
- → Proceed to **`NCA_Apex_Delivery_SOP.md`** (the engagement loop).

## 8. Open decisions index
- Points marked `⟨PENDING⟩` map to `NCA_Apex_Open_Decisions.md`. As the group decides, update this SOP and drop the flag.
