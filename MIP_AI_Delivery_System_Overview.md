## The program at a glance
A client moves through: **Kickstart** → **Pathfinder** → **Outpost** → **Frontier** → **Apex**.
- **Kickstart** — a ~90-min intro workshop delivered from the NCA training platform; shows the value, sets safe-use basics.
- **Pathfinder (Foundation)** — starts with the **AI Readiness Assessment** (included in sign-up), then a governance managed-service: clean/govern data, activate Copilot for first users.
- **Outpost (Adoption)** — a consulting engagement: working group, monthly AI-Expert session, first agents.
- **Frontier (Scale-Readiness)** — graduates the client from Copilot to Claude + other third-party AI, gets their third-party platforms inventoried and triaged for integration, and stands up the governance groundwork an autonomous system needs before anything unattended goes live.
- **Apex (Custom Build & Optimization)** — engagement-based custom AI agent/solution development and integration, a managed digital workforce, and ongoing optimization and roadmap planning.

## The spine — one journey, two rails
Pathfinder/Outpost/Frontier/Apex are **positions** on one journey (Foundation → Adoption → Scale-Readiness → Custom Build). Underneath, two kinds of work advance together:
- **Rail A — AI Capability:** what the team can *do* with AI (fluency → workflow thinking → builds → agents).
- **Rail B — Data & Process Foundation:** getting data and processes *ready*.

Rail B **paces** Rail A; they hand off at governance **gates**. Rules that hold everywhere:
- **Copilot carries Pathfinder & Outpost; Claude + third-party AI arrive at Frontier; custom builds happen at Apex.**
- **Start anywhere, but complete the foundation as catch-up — never skip it** (tracked on a Foundation Catch-Up Backlog).
- **Attended vs. unattended** AI is the governance dividing line — autonomous ("Co-work") AI triggers a heavier gate.

## How each stage is documented
Every stage carries an **Overview** (+ its deliverables list), an **Onboarding SOP**, a **Delivery SOP**, and an **Open Decisions register**. Shared tools (pull-menu, worksheets, trackers, templates) sit in **Reference** and are drawn on across stages.

## How decisions get made
Each **Open Decisions register** feeds the **weekly coordination group**. We draft the questions and options; **the group decides and assigns** — no owners are pre-assigned in any doc.

## Pricing — one model, every stage
There's no separate price tag per stage anymore. **One AI Consulting Pricing Model** prices all paid work: a flat monthly base + a per-user fee (every M365 tenant user) + consulting time priced by the size of the client's AI consulting group and how often it meets (monthly / biweekly / weekly). What changes stage to stage is the kind of work and deliverable — not the price mechanism. Only the total monthly price is shown to the client; no line-item breakdown. **Apex custom-build engagements are scoped and quoted per SOW on top of the standing price** — the formula prices the ongoing relationship, not one-off builds ⟨confirm with the group⟩.
