## What this stage is
- **Position:** the **Adoption** stage. Readiness **Level 4**.
- **Character:** a **consulting engagement** — a client working group, a recurring monthly AI-Expert session, and hands-on experiments.
- **Tool:** Copilot (Claude arrives at Frontier).
- **Delivery role:** an **AI Expert** (fractional).
- **Goal:** turn Copilot into a team habit and build the client's first agents — while the data foundation keeps catching up underneath.

## What happens
- Stand up / support the **working group** (sponsor, champions, testers — all need Copilot licenses).
- The monthly **4-beat session** (what landed · unblock · what's next · one new thing) and the **pull-menu** of experiments.
- The arc: Copilot Fluency → Workflow Thinking → First Builds → Prove & Productionize.
- Mostly **Rail A** capability work (stages A3–A5) + Rail B process documentation (B4).
