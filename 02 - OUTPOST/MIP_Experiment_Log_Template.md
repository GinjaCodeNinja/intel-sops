# Experiment Log — "What Landed"
## AI Discovery Journey — Working Group

**How to use:** One row per experiment. Keep it fast — a few minutes after each try. This log does double duty: it drives the monthly session with your NCI advisor, and it produces the value story leadership wants to see. Don't over-think the estimates; a rough "saved ~2 hrs/week" is far better than nothing.

## What "productionize" means
When an experiment keeps landing and people start relying on it, it's a candidate to graduate from *play* to a *supported build* — with a real data connection, an owner, guardrails, and monitoring. Flag it here and raise it in the monthly session; your advisor runs the graduation gate.
