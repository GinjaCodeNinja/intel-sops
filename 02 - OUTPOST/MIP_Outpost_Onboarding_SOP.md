# Outpost — Onboarding SOP *(placeholder)*
## AI Discovery Journey · Outpost Stage · For the AI Expert

**Status:** Internal Working Draft v0.1 — **placeholder.** EOS-style (20/80, point form). Points marked `⟨PENDING → Register §X⟩` await the weekly group — see `NCA_Outpost_Open_Decisions.md`.
**What this covers:** the **one-time setup** that gets an Outpost client from "signed up" to "ready for delivery." It produces the state the **Delivery SOP** (`NCA_Outpost_Delivery_SOP.md`) assumes — a stood-up working group, an agreed first focus, a booked cadence, and open tracking.
**Boundary:** Onboarding ends when the Kickoff is done and the client is delivery-ready. The first working session (Session 1) belongs to the Delivery SOP.
**Stance:** Consult-construct — we set them up to succeed; they own the decisions.

---

## 1. Purpose & boundary
- Get a new Outpost client from *signed* to *delivery-ready*, consistently, so no engagement starts vague.
- One-time (repeats only if a client re-onboards). The monthly rhythm lives in the Delivery SOP.
- Who runs onboarding: AI Expert, AM, or a coordinator? ⟨PENDING → Register §I1⟩

## 2. Trigger & handoff-in
- Trigger: client signs into Outpost (from a Pathfinder graduation or a direct/jumped entry).
- Receive the handoff from the AM/sales with account context (who they are, what they've done, the warm-lead history). ⟨PENDING intake point → Register §A2, §I1⟩
- Confirm the primary client contact and decision-maker.

## 3. Confirm preconditions *(don't start setup without these)*
- **Commercial:** MSA addendum / Outpost scope signed. ⟨PENDING → Register §B3⟩
- **Entry criteria** met, or the entry path agreed (earned from Pathfinder, or jumped-in with a catch-up backlog). ⟨PENDING → Register §B1, §B2⟩
- **Governance minimum** in place, or a **Foundation Catch-Up Backlog** opened for anything outstanding (framework §3). *For a jumped-ahead client this is where the backlog gets created.*

## 4. Provisioning & access
- Confirm Copilot licensing — **every member of the AI Working Group needs a full Copilot license.** They can't cut their teeth on Copilot without one. Confirm the client has enough seats and understands the per-seat cost scales with working-group size — and that the same working-group size is a direct input to the AI Consulting Pricing Model's consulting-hours term.
- Confirm what NCI needs to touch and get access agreed (tenant/workspace scope, tools). ⟨PENDING checklist → Register §I3⟩
- Confirm the client's Claude/third-party status is noted (and that supported Claude work is a Frontier item, not Outpost).

## 5. Stand up the working group
- Confirm/appoint the roles: **executive sponsor**, one or two **champions**, rotating **testers** from different departments (the domain experts).
- Agree size and department coverage. ⟨PENDING composition/size → Register §I4⟩
- **Licensing prerequisite:** every working-group member must have a Copilot license (see §4) — no license, no hands-on Copilot. Confirm seats are assigned before the group's first experiments.
- Give the group its operating basics: cadence between our sessions, a shared backlog, and the **pull-menu** (experiment cards).
- Client-facing support for this: do we hand them an **"AI Working Group" guide/one-pager**, and/or a **short training lesson** on running experiments? ⟨PENDING → Register §I5, §I6⟩ *(a `Working Group operating guide` already exists as an internal reference in the framework — this decides whether a client-facing version is needed.)*

## 6. Set up tracking & records
- Open the **readiness tracker** (`NCA_Data_Process_Readiness_Tracker.md`) for this client — Rail B milestones + any catch-up backlog.
- Open the **experiment log** (`NCA_Experiment_Log_Template.md`); agree who keeps it. ⟨PENDING → Register §F1⟩
- Set where the client record lives (IT Glue / SharePoint list / spreadsheet for now).

## 7. Run the Kickoff session *(the last step of onboarding)* ⟨PENDING whether/shape → Register §C1, §C2⟩
- **Goal:** everyone knows what Outpost is, what happens, and what "good" looks like.
- Attendees: sponsor, champions, AI Expert, AM (which are required vs. optional ⟨PENDING → Register §C2⟩).
- Draft agenda (~45–60 min):
  - Where they are on the journey — walk the **Journey Map**.
  - What Outpost is: the monthly rhythm, the working-group model, consult-construct boundaries.
  - Confirm the working group and roles.
  - Pick the **first focus** (a workflow, a Copilot habit, a first experiment).
  - Set the recurring session + confirm who books it. ⟨PENDING → Register §D1⟩
- Output: a short "here's our plan" recap to the client.

## 8. Set cadence & booking
- Default monthly; note if the client wants accelerated/bi-weekly (route per Delivery SOP §7 / Register §D3) — the AI Consulting Pricing Model prices this automatically via its frequency term, no separate add-on to negotiate.
- Create the recurring invite; confirm owner. ⟨PENDING → Register §D1⟩

## 9. Ready-for-Delivery checklist *(the handoff to the Delivery SOP)*
- [ ] Commercial signed; entry path agreed
- [ ] Governance minimum in place OR catch-up backlog opened
- [ ] Sponsor + at least one champion named; working group stood up
- [ ] Every working-group member has a Copilot license assigned
- [ ] Tracker + experiment log open; record location set
- [ ] First focus picked
- [ ] Cadence booked; session owner confirmed
- → Proceed to **Delivery SOP §4 (Session 1)**.

## 10. Open decisions index
- Points marked `⟨PENDING⟩` above map to `NCA_Outpost_Open_Decisions.md`. As the group decides, update this SOP and drop the flag.
