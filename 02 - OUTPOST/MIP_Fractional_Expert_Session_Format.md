# Fractional AI Expert — Monthly Session Format
## AI Discovery Journey — Outpost Stage Delivery

**Status:** Internal Working Draft v0.1
**Purpose:** Give the monthly advisory hour (Outpost stage) a consistent, repeatable shape so it's easy to sell and easy to deliver as the team scales. Fills the monthly-consulting-session-format gap from the May 2026 State of Play.
**Model:** Consult-construct. We advise, design, and de-risk. The client explores, decides, and owns. We do not make business decisions for them.

---

## The standing shape (~60 min)

The session flexes by where the client is on the spine, but every session has the same four beats:

| Beat | Time | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **1. What landed** | ~15 min | Walk the Experiment Log. What worked, what didn't, what surprised them. This is the value story — capture the one-sentence win for leadership. |
| **2. Unblock** | ~15 min | Whatever they're stuck on — a prompt that won't behave, an agent that misfires, "should this be AI or just a macro?" Hands-on where useful. |
| **3. What's next** | ~15 min | Suggest the next few experiment cards for where they are. Coach the concept behind them, not just the clicks. |
| **4. One new thing** | ~10 min | The standing **Emerging Capabilities** item — one new AI feature worth knowing about, and whether it's worth them trying. (See below.) |

Close by confirming: what will the working group try before we meet again, and is anything waiting on a governance gate?

---

## Per-stage emphasis

The four beats stay; the weight shifts with the client's stage.

- **Stage 2 — Copilot Fluency:** Beat 3 dominates. Drive real daily-use habits across the org before anyone chases agents. Watch for the "working group races to agents while the org still can't get a good summary" trap.
- **Stage 3 — Workflow Thinking:** Run a **Workflow Deconstruction** together on one of *their* real workflows. Teach the AI/Deterministic/Human triage live. This is the highest-leverage session in the whole journey.
- **Stage 4 — First Builds:** Beat 1 (what landed) and Beat 2 (unblock) dominate. Keep experiments small and safe; celebrate throwaways as learning.
- **Stage 5 — Prove & Productionize:** Scope a productionization; run the **graduation gate** (owner, data access, guardrails, measured benefit). Natural Outpost→Frontier conversation.
- **Stage 6 — Extend & Orchestrate:** Design integrations/agents. Run the **heavier gate** for anything autonomous. MCP / Claude / multi-agent work.

## The Emerging Capabilities standing item

Every session includes one "here's something new" segment. This is where we earn the "keeps us current" value of the subscription. Sourced from our own **New Capability Evaluation** work (`NCA_New_Capability_Evaluation_Template.md`) — we've already sandboxed and risk-assessed it, so we bring a verdict, not just news:
- *"Here's what it does. Here's whether it's safe for you yet. Here's whether it's worth your time."*
Survivors become new experiment cards.

---

## Deliverables from each session (lightweight)
- Updated Experiment Log roll-up (client keeps; we review)
- The one-sentence leadership win
- Agreed next experiments
- Any gate actions flagged
- Notes captured in the client's record (outcome language — what changed for them)

## Add-on hours
Additional recurring advisory hours are priced the same way as the standing consulting time — through the **AI Consulting Pricing Model**'s $200/hr consulting rate, not a separate add-on number. Use the same four-beat shape; deeper builds and integrations naturally consume more time and are where extra hours or Apex-stage scoping come in.
